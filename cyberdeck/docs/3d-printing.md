# 3D Printing Specs

You don't need a 3D printer — just send these specs to someone who has one. A local makerspace, a friend, or a printing service can turn this around in 1-2 days.

## Parts to Print

| Part | Qty | Dimensions | Est. Print Time |
|------|-----|-----------|----------------|
| Main body (bottom shell) | 1 | 220 × 140 × 25mm | ~6 hrs |
| Top lid | 1 | 220 × 140 × 12mm | ~3 hrs |
| Screen bezel | 2 | 130 × 85 × 4mm | ~1 hr each |
| Button cap | 6 | 14 × 14 × 6mm | ~20 min total |

## What to Tell Your Printer

> "I need these printed in PETG, 0.2mm layer height, 20% gyroid infill, 3 perimeter walls. Supports are only needed for the button holes in the main body. Can you do natural/black/dark green?"

## Recommended Settings

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Material | PETG (preferred) or PLA+ |
| Layer height | 0.2mm |
| Infill | 20% gyroid |
| Perimeters | 3 walls |
| Print temp (PETG) | 235°C nozzle / 70°C bed |
| Print temp (PLA+) | 215°C nozzle / 60°C bed |
| Supports | Yes, for button holes in main body only |
| Fan cooling | 50% for PETG / 100% for PLA+ |

## Enclosure Design Notes

The enclosure is designed around:
- Raspberry Pi 5 footprint (85 × 56mm), M2.5 mounting holes
- Two 5" screens (118 × 70mm active area) in the lid
- 6× 12mm button cutouts on the right side panel
- Micro-HDMI ports accessible from the back edge
- USB-A ports accessible from the left side
- USB-C power input on the right side
- Ventilation slots on bottom and sides
